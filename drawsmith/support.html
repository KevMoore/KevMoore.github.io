What DrawSmith is

DrawSmith simulates lottery draw machines with real physics, keeps your lines, checks them against official results and shows statistics. It does not sell tickets, and it cannot change your chances: every draw is random, and every line has the same odds.

  • Pro removes all ads, unlocks the Pro machine (camera modes, slow-motion replay, AR) and the full analysis.

Common questions

  • Results are missing or late: results show after the operator publishes them, usually on the evening of the draw. Pull down on the Results tab to check again.
  • My lines are not on my other device: turn on iCloud for DrawSmith on both devices, in Settings › your name › iCloud.
  • Ad privacy choices: open About and help › Ad privacy choices.
  • AR view shows no machine: point the camera at a flat, well-lit table or floor, and move the device slowly.
  • Delete your data: delete the app. To delete synced lines too, turn off iCloud for DrawSmith first. See the privacy policy.
